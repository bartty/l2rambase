Hunter G

    
   
Hunter G is a vote reward system controled by GM. You can aquire a special experience by killing monsters in Dimensional Rifts. When you kill more monster more experience percent are added to your character, but when you get maximum value of 200% your bonus is reseted to 0. The second name of that reward is REST EXPERIENCE. But remember you can only enter to rifts in party when you get Dimensional Items and you are registered in Seven Signs.
