Hallowed You

    
   
Adventurers must kill the spirits that come out from the bodies of living creatures in a cemetery in Aden or the Forest of Dead. For killing the spirit we receive the prize, a lollipop. Lollipops can be used to regenerate vitality or exchange to special prize in various NPC.
Rewards:
Vitality, Halloween Hats
   
   
Event Date: 1-30 November
