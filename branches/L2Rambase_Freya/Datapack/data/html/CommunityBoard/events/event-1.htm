Master of Enchanting

    
   
Collect Master Yogi's enchant scrolls. When you have obtained several scrolls, visit Master Yogi and purchase a Staff of Master Yogi. Use the event scrolls to enchant the staff and see how powerful you can make it! The higher you enchant the staff, the greater the reward that Master Yogi will have for you. Master Yogi's enchant scrolls will be available for sale by Master Yogi. You may purchase 24 enchant scrolls but only once every 6 hours (per account). If you don't want to wait for another 6 hours or more scrolls, you can purchase additional scrolls. Once you have enchanted the staff as much as you dare, give Master Yogi the staff for one of many rewards.
Rewards:
Firework,Large Firework,Shadow Item - Top Hat,Shadow Item - Black Mask,Shadow Item - Rider Goggles,Scroll: Enchant Weapon (D,C,B,A,S),Scroll: Enchant Armor (D,C,B,),Event - Top Hat,Event - Black Mask,Event - Rider Goggles,Grade S Accessory Chest (Event),Top-Grade Life Stone: level 76,Grade S Armor Chest (Event),Grade S Weapon Chest (Event),Red Soul Crystal - Stage 14
